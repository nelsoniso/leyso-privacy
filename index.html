Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

Short version: Leyso does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your location stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

1. Who we are

Leyso is a free map application showing sun-exposed café and restaurant terrasses in Geneva, Switzerland. It is produced and published by Avisarem. Contact: contact@avisarem.ch

2. Data we collect

Leyso collects no personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or any identifiers.
  • We do not track usage, install analytics, or use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. Location

Leyso can show your current position on the map. This is entirely optional. If you grant permission, your location is used only on your device to place a marker on the map — it is never sent to any server, stored, or logged anywhere. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings app.

4. Notifications

Leyso may send you local notifications to let you know when a sunny terrasse is available near you. These notifications are generated entirely on your device. No data leaves your device to produce them. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app.

5. Map and terrasse data

All terrasse and map data is bundled inside the app or fetched from public open-data sources:

  • SITG (State of Geneva) — terrasse permits, building footprints, street network (open data)
  • OpenStreetMap — supplementary names and links (ODbL)
  • Open-Meteo — current weather icon (CC-BY 4.0, no personal data sent)

When the weather icon is shown, Leyso fetches the current weather for Geneva's coordinates from Open-Meteo's public API. No personal information is included in this request.

6. Directions

Tapping "Y aller" opens Apple Maps (on iOS) or maps.apple.com (on the web) with the terrasse coordinates pre-filled. Leyso does not control what Apple does with that information; please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Children

Leyso does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Since we collect no personal data, changes are expected to be minor and infrequent.

9. Contact

Questions? Write to us at contact@avisarem.ch.
