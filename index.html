Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Short version: Leyso does not require an account and does not use analytics or advertising. Your location is never sent to our servers. If you optionally tick terrace tags (“Sur place”), those tags are shared with other users via our server — without your name or email.

1. Who we are

Leyso is a free map application showing sun-exposed café and restaurant terrasses in Geneva, Switzerland. It is produced and published by Avisarem. Contact: contact.avisarem@gmail.com

2. What we do not collect

Leyso does not build a profile of you. Specifically:

  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track usage, install analytics, or use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell personal data.

The one exception is optional terrace tags you choose to submit — see section 4 below.

3. Location

Leyso can show your current position on the map. This is entirely optional. If you grant permission, your location is used on your device to place a marker on the map and for nearby search — it is never sent to our servers. Your last known position may be saved locally on your device so the map can restore it on the next launch. You can revoke location permission at any time in your device's Settings app.

4. Terrace tags (“Sur place”)

Leyso lets you tick optional features on a terrasse (parasol, Wi‑Fi, dogs allowed, table count, etc.). This is crowd-sourced information shared with other users.

What is sent to our server What is not sent
An internal terrasse identifier Your name, email, or Apple/Google account
The tags you selected Your precise GPS position
A server timestamp Device ID or advertising identifier
  • Tags are stored on Cloudflare (Worker + database) and visible to all Leyso users; the latest change wins for a given terrasse.
  • We do not publish who changed a tag.
  • You can make up to 15 tag changes per Geneva calendar day per device (enforced locally).
  • A local cache and offline queue on your device sync when you are back online.

To request removal of tags for a specific terrasse, email us with the terrasse name and, if possible, a link from the app.

5. Data stored on your device

Leyso saves some preferences locally in your browser or app storage (for example favorites, language, theme, recent searches, notification settings, and the terrace-tag cache above). This data stays on your device unless you delete the app or clear site data.

6. Notifications

Leyso may send you local notifications to let you know when a sunny terrasse is available near you. These notifications are generated on your device from local data and settings. No personal data is sent to our servers to produce them. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings app.

7. Map and network services

Terrasse and map data is bundled inside the app or fetched from public sources and our infrastructure:

  • SITG (State of Geneva) — terrasse permits, building footprints, street network (open data)
  • OpenStreetMap — supplementary names and links (ODbL)
  • Open-Meteo — weather icon and forecasts for Geneva (CC-BY 4.0; Geneva coordinates only, no account)
  • swisstopo — address search via geo.admin.ch when you type in the search bar (your search query only)
  • Cloudflare — terrasse catalogue updates and terrace-tag sync (see section 4)

8. Owner / terrasse correction form

If you use the owner contact form to add or correct a terrasse, Leyso prepares an email in your mail app. You choose whether to send it. The message may include business details you enter (name, address, hours, phone, website, optional tags). We process those emails manually; nothing is uploaded without your send action.

9. Directions

Tapping “Y aller” opens Apple Maps (on iOS) or maps.apple.com (on the web) with the terrasse coordinates pre-filled. Leyso does not control what Apple does with that information; please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy.

10. In-app tips (iOS)

Optional tips use Apple In-App Purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive your card details.

11. Children

Leyso does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

We update this page when the app changes in ways that affect privacy. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.

13. Contact

Questions or deletion requests? Write to us at contact.avisarem@gmail.com.
